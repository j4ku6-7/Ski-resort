Piste angle & grading

The run steers across the hillside so the natural slope drops at exactly this angle. The steeper the hill, the more it traverses.

Bulldozers cut and fill along the run so it is never steeper than the angle you pick, and level it side to side across the hill. Earthworks cost €0.50 per m³. The stations keep their height, so a steep straight line can't be graded gentle; traverse instead.

Blue ≤ 14° (25%) · Red ≤ 22° (40%) · Black steeper. Without grading, the run follows the natural slope.

Slower and fewer carriers cut running costs but lower capacity.
